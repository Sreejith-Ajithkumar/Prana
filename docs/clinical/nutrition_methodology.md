# Prana Nutrition Methodology (Sprint 4)

## Purpose
Evidence-based calculations used in Prana v0.4.0.

## BMI
BMI = weight (kg) / height² (m²)
Categories: Underweight <18.5, Healthy 18.5-24.9, Overweight 25-29.9, Obesity ≥30.

## BMR
Mifflin-St Jeor:
Male = 10×weight + 6.25×height − 5×age + 5
Female = 10×weight + 6.25×height − 5×age − 161

## TDEE
BMR × Activity Multiplier
Sedentary 1.20
Lightly Active 1.375
Moderately Active 1.55
Very Active 1.725
Athlete 1.90

## Calories
Lose Weight: TDEE−500
Maintain: TDEE
Gain Muscle: TDEE+250
Improve Health: TDEE

## Protein
Lose:2.0 g/kg
Maintain:1.4 g/kg
Gain:2.2 g/kg
Improve:1.2 g/kg

## Fat
Lose 30%
Maintain 30%
Gain 25%
Improve 30%

Carbohydrates = Remaining calories.

## Water
0.033 L × weight (kg).

## Notes
Estimates for healthy adults; future versions will become adaptive.
